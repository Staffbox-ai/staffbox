# Synonyms: what customers write, and what we mean

Your customers' shorthand, written once so the worker reads it the way your counter does. Before pricing a request, the quote action rewrites the left column into the right column (longest phrase first; `-`, `/` and spaces are interchangeable, a trailing `s` is allowed). Several spellings go in one cell, separated by commas. Leave out anything that could mean two items: the worker should ask about those.

Example rows (delete them): `| blk, bk | black |` · `| 2p | 2-pole |` · `| sd 24x30 | shaker door 24x30 |`
