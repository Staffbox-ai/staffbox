# Ponce Electric Supply

A fictional example company used to demo and test Staffbox. It is not a real business. Its part numbers are its own house codes, not any manufacturer's.

- What we sell: electrical supplies to the trade: building wire and cable, breakers and load centers, boxes and devices, conduit and fittings, LED fixtures.
- Where: one branch with a will-call counter on Ponce de Leon Avenue, Atlanta, Georgia.
- Customers: electrical contractors, builders, maintenance departments, and some walk-in homeowners.
- The worker reports to the inside sales manager. Price exceptions go to the branch manager; credit and accounts go to the credit manager. See [[people]].
- The worker's first three workflows: price a line ([[quote-a-line]]), answer a policy question ([[answer-a-policy-question]]), route an inbound email ([[route-an-email]]).
- Rule: if the answer is not in this vault, say so and name who to ask. Never guess a price, a stock level, a date or a policy.
