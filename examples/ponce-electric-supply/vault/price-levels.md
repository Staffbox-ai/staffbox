# Price levels

- **List**: the price in the price book. Walk-in customers and anyone without an approved contractor account pay list.
- **Contractor level**: 12% off list, for approved contractor accounts only ([[accounts-and-credit]]). It applies to every stocked line, wire included, after the quantity break ([[quantity-breaks]]) and before the copper surcharge ([[copper-surcharge]]).
- Anything below contractor level is a price exception: only the branch manager approves it ([[people]]).
- Only use contractor level when the request says the customer is on a contractor account or asks for contractor pricing. Otherwise quote list.
