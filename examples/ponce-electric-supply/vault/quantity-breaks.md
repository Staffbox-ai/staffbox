# Quantity breaks (on the line total)

Counted in the item's own unit: each, stick or box (a box of 100 wire nuts counts as 1). Wire, cable and flex sold by the foot get no quantity break.

| Quantity on the line | Discount |
|---|---|
| 200 or more | 10% |
| 50 to 199 | 5% |
| under 50 | none |

Breaks apply per line, not per order. Contractor accounts get the break first, then their price level: [[price-levels]].
