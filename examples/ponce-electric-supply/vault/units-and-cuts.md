# Units, reels, coils and cuts

- Unit is in the price book's Unit column: **ft** (per foot), **ea** (each), **stick** (one 10 ft stick of conduit), **box of N** (sold by the box only).
- Wire and cable: priced per foot. Full reels are 500 ft (THHN, SER); full coils are 250 ft or 125 ft as the description says.
- A wire or cable length that is not a whole number of full reels or coils is cut from a reel. **Minimum cut: 25 feet**, whole feet only. **Cut charge: $7.50 per cut line**, added after the copper surcharge, never discounted.
- Conduit: whole 10 ft sticks only. A request in feet is converted to sticks (feet ÷ 10); if that is not a whole number, ask the customer.
- Box items: a request in pieces is converted to boxes (pieces ÷ box size); if that is not a whole number, ask the customer. Boxes are never broken.
- Flex (FMC): per foot from a 100 ft coil, no cut charge.
- If a request gives no length or no quantity, ask. Never assume a length.
