# Copper surcharge

The surcharge is a percent of the copper wire or cable line after any price level, rounded to the cent. It is never discounted.

| Product group | Surcharge | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| copper wire and cable (THHN, NM-B, UF-B, MC) | 8% | 1 October 2026 until further notice |
| copper wire and cable, before that | 5% | 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026 |
| aluminum cable (SER), conduit, flex, boxes, breakers, devices, fixtures | none | |

Quotes written today use the rate in effect today (8%). A quote written before 1 October 2026 is honored at its old rate for 30 days from its date; anything older is re-quoted.
