# Will-call counter

- Hours: Monday to Friday 6:30 am to 4:30 pm; Saturday 7:00 am to noon; closed Sunday.
- Orders called or emailed in by 2:00 pm are ready the same day; later orders are ready by 7:00 am the next business day.
- Wire cuts are made at the counter; minimum cut and cut charge: [[units-and-cuts]].
- We hold a will-call order for 5 business days, then return it to stock.
