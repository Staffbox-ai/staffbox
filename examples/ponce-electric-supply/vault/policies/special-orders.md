# Special orders

- Items marked "special order" in the price book, and anything not in the price book, are not stocked. The worker never prices them and never substitutes a stocked item; it sends them to the inside sales manager.
- Deposit: 50% at order, balance on pickup or delivery.
- Special-order items cannot be returned ([[returns]]).
- Lead times are quoted by the inside sales manager, item by item.
