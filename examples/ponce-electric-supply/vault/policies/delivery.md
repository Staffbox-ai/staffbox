# Delivery

- Inside I-285: free delivery on orders of $500 or more before tax; orders under $500 pay a $35 delivery fee.
- Orders in by 2:00 pm deliver the next business day.
- Outside I-285: not priced in this vault. Ask the warehouse lead.
