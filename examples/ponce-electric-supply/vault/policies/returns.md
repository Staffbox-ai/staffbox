# Returns

- Stocked items: within 30 days, unused, in the original packaging, with the invoice number.
- Restocking fee: 20% of the returned value.
- Cut wire and special-order items cannot be returned.
- Defective or dead on arrival: no restocking fee; route the email to the returns queue ([[route-an-email]]).
