# Accounts and credit

- Contractor accounts need an approved credit application and a copy of the contractor's state license.
- Terms on approved accounts: net 30 from the invoice date.
- Credit limit changes, statements and invoice questions go to the credit manager.
- Contractor price level: [[price-levels]].
