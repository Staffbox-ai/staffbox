# SOP: price a line

1. Find the SKU in the price book ([[price-wire]], [[price-breakers-panels]], [[price-boxes-devices]], [[price-conduit]], [[price-lighting]]). If it is not there, stop: answer NOT IN VAULT and send it to the inside sales manager. If its Stock column says special order, do not price it: answer NOT IN VAULT, special order, ask the inside sales manager ([[special-orders]]).
2. Put the quantity in the item's unit ([[units-and-cuts]]): feet for wire, sticks for conduit, boxes for box items. If the length or quantity is missing or does not convert to whole sticks or boxes, ask the customer.
3. Goods = quantity × list price. Round to the cent.
4. Quantity break from [[quantity-breaks]] (not for items sold by the foot). Round to the cent.
5. Contractor account only: take 12% off ([[price-levels]]). Round to the cent.
6. Copper wire and cable only: add the copper surcharge in effect today, 8% ([[copper-surcharge]]). Round to the cent.
7. Wire cut from a reel or coil (not a whole number of full reels or coils): add the $7.50 cut charge.
8. Answer with the line total and show the working in one line. Several lines: price each line, then add them up.

Worked example 1: 60 × LT-DL6 (6in LED retrofit downlight), list. 60 × $11.50 = $690.00; 60 is 50 to 199 → 5% → $655.50.

Worked example 2: 300 ft of W-NM12-2 (NM-B 12/2), contractor account. 300 × $0.78 = $234.00; no quantity break on wire; contractor −12% → $205.92; copper +8% → $222.39; 300 ft is not a whole number of 250 ft coils → cut charge $7.50 → $229.89.
