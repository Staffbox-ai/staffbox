# Delivery

One delivery fee per order, by zone, added after all lines are priced. It is never discounted.

| Zone | Areas | Fee per delivery |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brookhaven, Chamblee, Doraville, Dunwoody, Buckhead | $55.00 |
| 2 | Midtown, Downtown, Decatur, Virginia-Highland, Kirkwood, West Midtown, Sandy Springs | $85.00 |
| 3 | Roswell, Marietta, Smyrna, Tucker, Norcross, Alpharetta, Lilburn | $135.00 |

- Free delivery in zones 1 and 2 on orders of $2,500.00 or more (the sum of the lines after account levels, market adjustment and cut charges). Zone 3 always pays its fee.
- An area not listed above is not priced in this vault: ask the dispatch lead. Never guess a zone.
- Orders in by 2:00 pm deliver the next business day. Deliveries are dropped at the curb or on the job-site ground; we do not carry material upstairs.
