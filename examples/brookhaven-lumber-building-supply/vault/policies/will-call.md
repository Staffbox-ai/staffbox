# Will-call yard

- Hours: Monday to Friday 6:30 am to 5:00 pm; Saturday 7:00 am to 12:00 noon; closed Sunday.
- Orders called or emailed in by 2:00 pm are pulled the same day; later orders are ready by 7:00 am the next business day.
- The yard crew loads with a forklift; the customer straps and flags the load. Trim cuts are made at the counter; minimum cut and cut charge: [[units-and-cuts]].
- We hold a will-call order for 5 business days, then return it to stock.
