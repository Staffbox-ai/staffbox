# Returns

- Stocked items: within 30 days, unused, in sellable condition, with the invoice number.
- Restocking fee: 20% of the returned value.
- Cut trim, opened boxes of fasteners, broken pallets of bagged goods and special-order items cannot be returned.
- Material damaged, warped or the wrong item on delivery, reported within 48 hours: no restocking fee; route the email to the returns queue ([[route-an-email]]).
