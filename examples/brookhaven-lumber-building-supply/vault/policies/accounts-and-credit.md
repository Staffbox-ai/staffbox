# Accounts and credit

- Contractor accounts need an approved credit application and a copy of the contractor's Georgia business license.
- Builder program: a contractor account that builds new homes, with a signed builder agreement and $400,000 or more of purchases in the trailing 12 months, can enroll. The credit manager reviews enrollment each quarter.
- Terms on approved accounts: net 30 from the invoice date.
- Credit limit changes, builder program enrollment, statements and invoice questions go to the credit manager.
