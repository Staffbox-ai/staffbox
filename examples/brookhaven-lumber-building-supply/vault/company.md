# Brookhaven Lumber & Building Supply (fictional)

A fictional example company used to demo and test Staffbox. It is not a real business. Its part numbers are its own house codes, not any manufacturer's, and its products are house-brand or unbranded commodity goods.

- What we sell: lumber and building materials to the trade: kiln-dried framing lumber, pressure-treated lumber, hardwoods and cedar by the board foot, plywood, OSB and drywall, fasteners and connectors, bagged concrete and mortar, trim and moulding, and doors and windows.
- Where: one lumber yard with a will-call counter in Brookhaven, in north Atlanta, Georgia.
- Customers: framing and remodeling contractors, deck builders, home builders on our builder program, property maintenance departments, and some walk-in homeowners.
- The worker reports to the inside sales manager. Price exceptions go to the general manager; doors and windows go to the millwork desk; credit and accounts go to the credit manager; deliveries go to the dispatch lead. See [[people]].
- The worker's first three workflows: price a line ([[quote-a-line]]), answer a policy question ([[answer-a-policy-question]]), route an inbound email ([[route-an-email]]).
- Rule: if the answer is not in this vault, say so and name who to ask. Never guess a price, a stock level, a date, a size, a length, a delivery zone or a policy.
