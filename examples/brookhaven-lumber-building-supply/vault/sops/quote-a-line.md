# SOP: price a line

1. Find the SKU in the price book ([[price-dimensional]], [[price-treated]], [[price-board-foot]], [[price-sheet-goods]], [[price-fasteners]], [[price-concrete]], [[price-trim]], [[price-doors-windows]]). Shorthand: [[synonyms]]. If it is not there, stop: answer NOT IN VAULT and send it to the inside sales manager. If its Stock column says special order, do not price it: answer NOT IN VAULT, special order ([[special-orders]]); doors and windows go to the millwork desk.
2. Put the quantity in the item's unit ([[units-and-cuts]]): pieces for lumber (the length must be given), board feet for HW- species, whole boxes for boxed fasteners, whole pallets plus loose bags for bagged goods with a pallet row, linear feet for trim. If the size, length, thickness, quantity or (prehung doors) handing is missing, or pieces do not convert to whole boxes, ask the customer.
3. Goods = quantity x list price. Round to the cent.
4. Quantity break from [[quantity-breaks]] (lumber by the bundle, board feet, sheets, fastener boxes and each). Round to the cent.
5. Account level, only when the request says so: contractor 8% off, or builder 12% off ([[price-levels]]). Never on a pallet row. Round to the cent.
6. Dimensional (DL-) and treated (PT-) lumber only: add the market adjustment in effect today ([[lumber-market-adjustment]]). Round to the cent.
7. Trim that is not a whole number of stocked lengths: add the $2.00 cut charge ([[units-and-cuts]]).
8. Several lines: price each line, then add them up.
9. Delivery: if the request asks for delivery, add the zone fee once for the order unless the order qualifies for free delivery ([[delivery]]). If the area is not in a zone, answer NOT IN VAULT for the delivery and send it to the dispatch lead.
10. Answer with the total and show the working in one line per line.

Worked example 1: 30 x SG-CDX12 (CDX plywood 1/2in), list. 30 x $32.40 = $972.00; 20 to 49 sheets → 3% → $942.84.

Worked example 2: 96 x DL-2810 (2x8 x 10 ft), contractor account. 96 x $11.48 = $1,102.08; 96 is a full 2x8 bundle → 7% → $1,024.93; contractor −8% → $942.94; market adjustment +5% → $990.09.

Worked example 3: 48 bags of CM-CON80 (concrete mix 80 lb), builder account. 1 pallet of 42 at $246.40 (net, no level) + 6 bags x $6.45 = $38.70, builder −12% → $34.06; total $280.46.

Worked example 4: 70 lf of TR-CAS312 (flat casing, 16 ft lengths), list. 70 x $1.65 = $115.50; 70 ft is not a whole number of 16 ft lengths → cut charge $2.00 → $117.50.
