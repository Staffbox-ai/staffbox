# SOP: route an inbound email

Put every email in exactly one queue.

| Queue | Use when the email |
|---|---|
| quote | asks for a price or availability on lumber, sheet goods, fasteners, concrete or trim |
| order_status | asks where a delivery, order or PO is, or when it will arrive (not doors or windows) |
| will_call | asks about a yard pickup: is it ready, can it be held, pickup times |
| returns | wants to send back an unused item, or reports material damaged, warped or wrong on delivery |
| millwork | is about doors or windows: a price, a lead time, a handing question or where a door or window order is. Millwork wins over quote and order_status. |
| credit | asks about an account, an account level or the builder program, a credit application or limit, a statement, an invoice or a payment |
| other | anything else: jobs, vendors, sales reps, events |
