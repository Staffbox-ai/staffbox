# Account levels

- **List**: the price in the price book. Walk-in customers and anyone without an approved account pay list.
- **Contractor level**: 8% off list, for approved contractor accounts ([[accounts-and-credit]]).
- **Builder level**: 12% off list, for accounts enrolled in the builder program ([[accounts-and-credit]]). It replaces the contractor level; the two never stack.
- Pallet rows of bagged concrete and mortar are already net: no account level on a pallet row. Loose bags do get the account level.
- Anything below a customer's account level is a price exception: only the general manager approves it ([[people]]).
- Only use an account level when the request says the customer is on a contractor or builder account, or asks for that pricing. Otherwise quote list.

## Rule order (every line, rounded to the cent after each step)

1. Goods = quantity x list price.
2. Quantity break ([[quantity-breaks]]).
3. Account level: contractor 8% or builder 12%.
4. Market adjustment, dimensional and treated lumber only ([[lumber-market-adjustment]]).
5. Trim cut charge ([[units-and-cuts]]).

Then, once per order: the delivery fee ([[delivery]]), on the sum of the lines.
