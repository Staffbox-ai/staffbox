# Quantity breaks (on the line total)

Breaks apply per line, not per order, and are counted in the item's own unit. Accounts with a level get the break first, then their account level: [[price-levels]].

| Items | Break |
|---|---|
| Dimensional (DL-) and treated (PT-) lumber, by the piece | one full bundle or more of that size (bundle sizes below): 7% |
| Board-foot species (HW-) | 200 board feet or more: 5% |
| Sheet goods (SG-) | 50 sheets or more: 6%; 20 to 49 sheets: 3% |
| Fasteners and connectors (FS-) sold by the box or each | 10 boxes or each or more: 5% |

No quantity break on:
- bulk nails sold by the pound;
- bagged concrete, mortar, sand, rebar and mesh (CM-): bags already have pallet pricing ([[price-concrete]]);
- trim sold by the linear foot (TR-);
- doors and windows (DW-).
