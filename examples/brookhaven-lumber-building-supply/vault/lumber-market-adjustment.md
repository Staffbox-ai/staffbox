# Lumber market adjustment

A percentage added to every dimensional (DL-) and treated (PT-) lumber line, after the account level, rounded to the cent. It is never discounted. It does not apply to board-foot species, sheet goods, fasteners, concrete, trim, or doors and windows.

| Applies to | Adjustment | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| dimensional lumber (DL-) | +5% | 12 October 2026 until further notice |
| treated lumber (PT-) | +3% | 12 October 2026 until further notice |
| dimensional lumber (DL-), before that | +2% | 1 September 2026 to 11 October 2026 |
| treated lumber (PT-), before that | none | to 11 October 2026 |

Quotes written today use the adjustment in effect today. A quote written before 12 October 2026 is honored at its old adjustment for 14 days from its date; anything older is re-quoted.
