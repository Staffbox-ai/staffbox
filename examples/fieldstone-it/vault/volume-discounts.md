# Volume discounts

Discounts apply to the **hardware part** of a line only. Setup fees are never discounted.

| Quantity on the line | Discount on hardware |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9 | none |
| 10 to 24 | 4% |
| 25 or more | 7% |
