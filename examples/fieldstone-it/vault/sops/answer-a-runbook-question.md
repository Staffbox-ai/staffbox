# SOP: answer a runbook or policy question

1. Find the note that covers it: runbooks under Runbooks, rules under Policies, client facts under Clients in [[Home]].
2. Answer in one or two sentences with the exact number, time or step from the note, and name the note.
3. If no note covers it, reply "Not in the vault" and name who to ask from [[people]].
