# SOP: route an inbound email

Put every email in exactly one queue.

| Queue | Use when the email |
|---|---|
| service_desk | reports something broken or not working: a device, printer, email, Wi-Fi, an app |
| quote | asks for a price on hardware or project work |
| security | reports a suspicious email, a possible phishing link, a hacked account or a lost device with company data |
| onboarding | announces a new starter or a leaver |
| billing | asks about an invoice, a payment or the monthly plan |
| other | anything else: jobs, vendors, events |
