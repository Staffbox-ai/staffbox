# Response times (managed clients)

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | whole office down, or a security incident | 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| P2 | one team or one key system down | 1 business hour |
| P3 | one person affected | 4 business hours |
| P4 | request or question | next business day |
