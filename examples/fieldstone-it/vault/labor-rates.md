# Labor rates

- Project work, business hours (Mon to Fri, 8 am to 6 pm): **$165 per hour**.
- After hours, weekends and holidays: **$225 per hour**, 2-hour minimum.
- Managed clients: help-desk tickets are included in the monthly plan; project work is billed at the rates above.
