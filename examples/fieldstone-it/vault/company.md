# Fieldstone IT

A fictional example company used to demo and test Staffbox. It is not a real business, and its clients are fictional too.

- What we do: managed IT for small businesses (help desk, devices, networks, backups, security) plus hardware and project work.
- Where: one office in Decatur, Georgia. Clients within the metro area.
- The worker reports to the service desk lead. Quotes over $10,000 and anything touching security go to the service director. See [[people]].
- The worker's first three workflows: price a hardware line ([[quote-a-hardware-line]]), answer a runbook or policy question ([[answer-a-runbook-question]]), route an inbound email ([[route-an-email]]).
- Rule: if the answer is not in this vault, say so and name who to ask. Never guess a price, a date, a client fact or a procedure.
