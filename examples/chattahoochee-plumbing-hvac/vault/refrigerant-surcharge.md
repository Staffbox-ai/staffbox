# Refrigerant surcharge

The surcharge is a fixed amount per pound on every refrigerant line, added after any price level, rounded to the cent. It is never discounted.

| Refrigerant | Surcharge per lb | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| R-410A | $3.25 | 1 October 2026 until further notice |
| R-410A, before that | $2.00 | 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026 |
| R-454B, R-32 | $0.85 | 1 October 2026 until further notice |
| R-22 (reclaimed) | special order, priced by the HVAC sales lead | |

Quotes written today use the rate in effect today. A quote written before 1 October 2026 is honored at its old rate for 30 days from its date; anything older is re-quoted.
