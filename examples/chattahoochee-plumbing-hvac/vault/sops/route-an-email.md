# SOP: route an inbound email

Put every email in exactly one queue.

| Queue | Use when the email |
|---|---|
| quote | asks for a price or availability on items or equipment |
| order_status | asks where a delivery, order or PO is, or when it will arrive |
| will_call | asks about a counter pickup: is it ready, can it be held, pickup times |
| returns | wants to send back an unused item, reports an item defective or dead on arrival before install, or a wrong item received |
| warranty | reports a water heater, condenser or air handler that failed after it was installed |
| credit | asks about an account, a price level or Trade Pro enrollment, a credit application or limit, a statement, an invoice or a payment |
| other | anything else: jobs, vendors, sales reps, events |
