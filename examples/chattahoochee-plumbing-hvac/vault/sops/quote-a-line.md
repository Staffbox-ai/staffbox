# SOP: price a line

1. Find the SKU in the price book ([[price-copper]], [[price-pex]], [[price-valves]], [[price-water-heaters]], [[price-hvac-equipment]], [[price-refrigerant]], [[price-filters]]). Shorthand: [[synonyms]]. If it is not there, stop: answer NOT IN VAULT and send it to the inside sales manager. If its Stock column says special order, do not price it: answer NOT IN VAULT, special order ([[special-orders]]).
2. HVAC: if the request has a condenser and an air handler, look them up in the matched-systems table ([[price-hvac-equipment]]). A matched pair is priced as its SYS- row. An unmatched pair is not priced: answer NOT IN VAULT, unmatched system, send it to the HVAC sales lead.
3. Put the quantity in the item's unit ([[units-and-cuts]]): feet for copper pipe and PEX, pounds in whole cylinders for refrigerant, bags or cases for bag and case items. If the size, length or quantity is missing, or does not convert to whole coils, cylinders, bags or cases, ask the customer.
4. Goods = quantity × list price. Round to the cent.
5. Quantity break from [[quantity-breaks]] (not for items sold by the foot or pound, and not for equipment). Round to the cent.
6. Price level, only when the request says so: contractor 10% off, or Trade Pro 15% off ([[price-levels]]). Round to the cent.
7. Refrigerant only: add the surcharge in effect today, per pound × pounds ([[refrigerant-surcharge]]). Round to the cent.
8. Copper pipe that is not a whole number of that SKU's sticks: add the $3.50 cut charge ([[units-and-cuts]]).
9. Answer with the line total and show the working in one line. Several lines: price each line, then add them up.

Worked example 1: 30 × FC-TE075 (copper tee 3/4in), list. 30 × $3.60 = $108.00; 30 is 25 to 99 → 4% → $103.68.

Worked example 2: 50 ft of CP-L100-20 (type L copper 1in, 20 ft stick), contractor account. 50 × $7.05 = $352.50; no quantity break on pipe; contractor −10% → $317.25; 50 ft is not a whole number of 20 ft sticks → cut charge $3.50 → $320.75.

Worked example 3: 40 lb of RF-454B-20 (R-454B, 20 lb cylinder), Trade Pro. 40 lb = 2 cylinders. 40 × $21.50 = $860.00; no quantity break on refrigerant; Trade Pro −15% → $731.00; surcharge 40 × $0.85 = $34.00 → $765.00.
