# Price levels

- **List**: the price in the price book. Walk-in customers and anyone without an approved contractor account pay list.
- **Contractor level**: 10% off list, for approved contractor accounts ([[accounts-and-credit]]).
- **Trade Pro level**: 15% off list, for contractor accounts enrolled in Trade Pro ([[accounts-and-credit]]). It replaces the contractor level; the two never stack.
- A price level applies to every stocked line, equipment and refrigerant included, after the quantity break ([[quantity-breaks]]) and before the refrigerant surcharge ([[refrigerant-surcharge]]) and the cut charge ([[units-and-cuts]]).
- Anything below a customer's price level is a price exception: only the branch manager approves it ([[people]]).
- Only use a price level when the request says the customer is on a contractor account or on Trade Pro, or asks for that pricing. Otherwise quote list.
