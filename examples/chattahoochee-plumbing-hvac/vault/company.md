# Chattahoochee Plumbing & HVAC Supply (fictional)

A fictional example company used to demo and test Staffbox. It is not a real business. Its part numbers are its own house codes, not any manufacturer's, and its products are house-brand.

- What we sell: plumbing and HVAC supplies to the trade: copper pipe and fittings, PEX and PEX fittings, valves, water heaters, split-system condensers and air handlers, refrigerant, line sets and air filters.
- Where: one branch with a will-call counter on the west side of Atlanta, Georgia.
- Customers: plumbing and HVAC contractors, builders, property maintenance departments, and some walk-in homeowners.
- The worker reports to the inside sales manager. Price exceptions go to the branch manager; HVAC systems go to the HVAC sales lead; credit and accounts go to the credit manager. See [[people]].
- The worker's first three workflows: price a line ([[quote-a-line]]), answer a policy question ([[answer-a-policy-question]]), route an inbound email ([[route-an-email]]).
- Rule: if the answer is not in this vault, say so and name who to ask. Never guess a price, a stock level, a date, a size, a length or a policy.
