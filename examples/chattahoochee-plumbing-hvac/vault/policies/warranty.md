# Warranty claims

- A water heater, condenser or air handler that failed after it was installed is a warranty claim. Route the email to the warranty queue ([[route-an-email]]).
- The claim needs the serial number, the install date and the invoice number.
- Warranty terms and lengths are not in this vault. The warranty coordinator answers them.
