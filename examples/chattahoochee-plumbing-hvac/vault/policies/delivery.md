# Delivery

- Inside I-285: free delivery on orders of $750 or more before tax; orders under $750 pay a $45 delivery fee.
- Orders in by 1:00 pm deliver the next business day.
- Equipment (water heaters, condensers, air handlers) is delivered to the curb or the job-site ground floor only. We do not set or carry equipment.
- Outside I-285: not priced in this vault. Ask the warehouse lead.
