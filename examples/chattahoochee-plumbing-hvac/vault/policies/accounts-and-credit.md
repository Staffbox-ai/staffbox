# Accounts and credit

- Contractor accounts need an approved credit application and a copy of the contractor's Georgia plumbing or conditioned-air license.
- Trade Pro: a contractor account with $250,000 or more of purchases in the trailing 12 months can enroll. The credit manager reviews enrollment each quarter.
- Terms on approved accounts: net 30 from the invoice date.
- Credit limit changes, Trade Pro enrollment, statements and invoice questions go to the credit manager.
