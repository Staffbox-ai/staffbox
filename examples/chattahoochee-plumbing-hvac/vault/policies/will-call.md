# Will-call counter

- Hours: Monday to Friday 6:00 am to 5:00 pm; Saturday 7:00 am to 11:00 am; closed Sunday.
- Orders called or emailed in by 1:00 pm are ready the same day; later orders are ready by 6:30 am the next business day.
- Copper cuts are made at the counter; minimum cut and cut charge: [[units-and-cuts]].
- Refrigerant is released only to a buyer whose refrigerant-handling certification card is on file with us.
- We hold a will-call order for 3 business days, then return it to stock.
