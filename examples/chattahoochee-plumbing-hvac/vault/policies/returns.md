# Returns

- Stocked items: within 30 days, unused, in the original packaging, with the invoice number.
- Restocking fee: 15% of the returned value.
- Cut copper, opened refrigerant cylinders and special-order items cannot be returned.
- Defective or dead on arrival (never installed): no restocking fee; route the email to the returns queue ([[route-an-email]]).
- Equipment that failed after it was installed is a warranty claim, not a return: [[warranty]].
