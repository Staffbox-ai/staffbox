# Units, sticks, coils, cylinders and cuts

- Unit is in the price book's Unit column: **ft** (per foot), **lb** (per pound), **ea** (each), **coil** (one coil of ACR tubing), **system** (one matched condenser + air handler), **bag of N** and **case of N** (sold by the bag or case only).
- Copper pipe: priced per foot, stocked in 10 ft and 20 ft sticks (separate SKUs). If the customer does not say 10 ft or 20 ft sticks, quote the 20 ft stick SKU. A length that is not a whole number of that SKU's sticks is cut from a stick. **Minimum cut: 2 feet**, whole feet only. **Cut charge: $3.50 per cut line**, added after the price level, never discounted.
- PEX tubing: priced per foot, sold by the whole coil only, never cut. The length must be a whole number of the named coil size. If the customer gives feet but no coil size: when the length equals one stocked coil length, quote one coil of that size; otherwise ask which coils.
- Refrigerant: priced per pound, sold by the full cylinder only. A request in pounds is converted to cylinders (pounds ÷ cylinder size); if that is not a whole number, ask the customer.
- Bag and case items: a request in pieces is converted to bags or cases (pieces ÷ bag or case size); if that is not a whole number, ask the customer. Bags and cases are never broken.
- If a request gives no size, no length or no quantity, ask. Never assume a size or a length.
