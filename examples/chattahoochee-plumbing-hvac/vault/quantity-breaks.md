# Quantity breaks (on the line total)

Counted in the item's own unit: each, bag, case or coil of ACR tubing (a bag of 100 crimp rings counts as 1).

No quantity break on:
- items priced by the foot (copper pipe, PEX tubing) or by the pound (refrigerant);
- equipment: water heaters (WH-), condensers (AC-), air handlers (AH-) and matched systems (SYS-).

| Quantity on the line | Discount |
|---|---|
| 100 or more | 8% |
| 25 to 99 | 4% |
| under 25 | none |

Breaks apply per line, not per order. Accounts with a price level get the break first, then their price level: [[price-levels]].
