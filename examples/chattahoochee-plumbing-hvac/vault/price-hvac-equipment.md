## Matched systems

A condenser and an air handler are a matched system only when they appear together on one row below. When a request has both halves of a matched system on it, price the system row, not the two pieces. A condenser and air handler that are not on one row below are an unmatched pairing: do not price the pair; answer NOT IN VAULT and send it to the HVAC sales lead. A condenser or an air handler asked for on its own (a replacement) is priced at its own row.
