# Peachtree Cabinet Works

A fictional example company used to demo and test Staffbox. It is not a real business.

- What we make: cabinet doors (shaker, slab, raised panel), dovetail drawer boxes, drawer fronts and crown moulding.
- Where: one plant in Marietta, Georgia.
- Customers: kitchen and bath remodelers, cabinet shops and general contractors.
- The worker reports to the office manager. Price exceptions and rush orders go to the plant manager. See [[people]].
- The worker's first three workflows: price a line ([[quote-a-line]]), answer a policy question ([[answer-a-policy-question]]), route an inbound email ([[route-an-email]]).
- Rule: if the answer is not in this vault, say so and name who to ask. Never guess a price, a date or a policy.
