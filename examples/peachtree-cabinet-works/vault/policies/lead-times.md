# Lead times

| Order finish | Lead time |
|---|---|
| unfinished | 10 business days |
| stained | 15 business days |
| painted | 20 business days |

An order with mixed finishes takes the longest lead time on it. Faster than this is a rush order: [[price-exceptions-and-rush]].
