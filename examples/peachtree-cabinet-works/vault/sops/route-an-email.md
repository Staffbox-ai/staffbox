# SOP: route an inbound email

Put every email in exactly one queue.

| Queue | Use when the email |
|---|---|
| quote | asks for a price or a number on items |
| order_status | asks where an order or PO is, or when it ships |
| complaint | reports damage, a defect, a wrong finish or anything out of spec |
| cert_request | asks for a certificate (FSC, CARB), a spec sheet or compliance paperwork |
| other | anything else: jobs, hours, tours, vendors |
