# SOP: price a line

1. Find the SKU's base unit price in [[price-list]]. If it is not there, stop: it is custom, send it to the plant manager.
2. Add the species surcharge and the finish surcharge from [[surcharges]]: unit = base × (1 + species% + finish%). Round to the cent.
3. Line total = unit × quantity.
4. Apply the volume discount from [[volume-discounts]] to the line total. Round to the cent.
5. Answer with the line total, and show the working in one line.

Worked example: 30 × SD-2430 (shaker door 24x30) in maple, painted.
Base $55.00; maple +10%, painted +20% → unit $55.00 × 1.30 = $71.50. 30 × $71.50 = $2,145.00. 30 units → 5% → $2,037.75.
