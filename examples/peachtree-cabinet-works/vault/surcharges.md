# Surcharges (percent of the base unit price)

Species and finish surcharges are both taken on the base unit price and added together. They do not compound.

| Species | Surcharge |
|---|---|
| poplar | 0% |
| maple | 10% |
| white oak | 18% |
| cherry | 25% |

| Finish | Surcharge |
|---|---|
| unfinished | 0% |
| stained | 12% |
| painted | 20% |

We stock only these four species. Walnut, hickory and anything else is custom: see [[price-list]].
