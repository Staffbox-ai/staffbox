# Volume discounts (on the line total)

| Quantity on the line | Discount |
|---|---|
| 50 or more units | 8% |
| 25 to 49 units | 5% |
| under 25 units | none |

Discounts apply per line, not per order. No other discount exists; any other request is a price exception ([[price-exceptions-and-rush]]).
